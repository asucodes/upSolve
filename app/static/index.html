Recommended problems

Why these problems?

These are uncalibrated difficulty-match estimates, not measured chances of solving independently. Warm-ups stay at or below your current rating band; main and stretch problems allow up to 100 and 200 additional rating points. Sparse topic evidence is pulled toward your current rating. Missing suitable problems are not replaced with much harder ones.
